# Score hidden; no persistent gamification surfaces

Line shows **no** streaks, points, badges, percentages, or "session complete" screens,
and the score is hidden entirely. Internal per-Setup tracking (for ladder position) is
fine; it is never surfaced. The only "reward" is the warmth of the Reveal — warm
affirmation on the Coached line, a genuine "even better" when Alex finds a valid
alternative, a gentle "here's the cleaner line, and why" on a Divergence.

## Why this is worth recording

This is an explicit *no* that a future contributor (or Carl, six months in) will be
tempted to reverse — "it's an app for a kid, add stars and streaks to keep him engaged."
The decision carries a real trade-off and constraints not visible in the code.

- Inherited discipline from Settle and Paths: surfaces convert a tool into a chore.
- Reconciles two forces in Carl's notes: the anti-gamification discipline *and* the
  parenting principle "praise cleverness, not routine compliance." The "even better" path
  is how the app praises cleverness — by rewarding *thinking*, not by awarding points.
- Engagement is meant to come from the teaching moment landing, and from Carl game-ifying
  verbally at the table, not from a meter in the app.

## Consequences

- The "even better" (valid-alternative) branch of the Reveal is load-bearing, not a nicety
  — it is the app's entire reward mechanism. It must be built, not deferred.
